About the Strategies
MA Crossover 20/50
Buy when 20-day SMA crosses above 50-day SMA. Classic trend-following used by CTA and macro funds.
RSI Mean Reversion
Buy RSI < 30 (oversold), sell RSI > 70. Statistical reversion used by stat arb desks.
Bollinger Bands
Buy at lower 2σ band, sell at mean. Rolling-volatility mean reversion used by vol arb desks.
Cross-Sectional Momentum
Buy top 20% 3-month performers. Jegadeesh & Titman (1993). Used by AQR, Two Sigma.
Pairs Trading
Long/short correlated pairs when spread diverges. Engle-Granger cointegration.
MACD Signal
EMA(12)−EMA(26) with 9-day signal. Exponential weighting favors recent momentum.
